# Aggregate figure data

All CSVs are UTF-8 with headers. Blank numeric fields mean unavailable or inapplicable, not zero. Rows preserve source order for plotting.

- **framework.csv**: panel, heading, detail. Editorial schematic text, not measured data.
- **prisma.csv**: key and count. Database and citation counts are kept separate. Publication-type/component/AF/population/ECG exclusions total 161.
- **performance.csv**: figure and panel select the display; label identifies model/site/reference; metric is Sensitivity, Specificity, or AUROC. value/lower/upper are proportions. interval_type is reported_95ci, wilson_95ci, cross_fold_sd, or none. numerator/denominator are aggregate binary counts only for Wilson reconstruction. comparator=1 flags a within-study comparator. source and analysis_unit retain provenance and denominator meaning. SD limits represent mean ± SD, not uncertainty intervals. No missing CI is inferred from a score.
- **quadas.csv / probast.csv**: one row per model/site/stage record, not per independent study. record_id, study_id, model, assessment_stage, and display_label identify the appraisal object. participants, index_test_or_predictors, target_condition_or_outcome, analysis, and overall contain bias or stage-specific quality judgments. app_* fields are applicability. Values are low, high, unclear, or insufficient information. No score is calculated from colors. Review-status administrative fields are intentionally omitted from plotting inputs.
- **grade.csv**: id is the retained profile identifier; study is a historical internal report key, not a citation number; outcome and metric identify the display target; value is the estimate; cert is the reconciled final category. starting_certainty and independent_studies retain context. Five *_deduction columns store numeric downgrades. Separate *_display fields hold 0/1/2, NA (not assessable), or U (undetermined). Never translate NA/U to a favorable judgment. Existing profile identifiers need not be consecutive.
- **sensitivity_scenarios.csv**: verbatim scenario label, counts in moderate/low/very-low order where estimable, changed/excluded profiles, and source. Non-estimable cases remain descriptive, not zero counts. Scenarios are independent applications of restrictions or rules, not cumulative.
- **references.csv**: current manuscript reference number and full bibliographic citation. Contextual/excluded studies in this list are not additional included studies.

Source snapshot: concise manuscript dated November 10, 2026; current Multimedia Appendices 5–8; reconciled Figure 5–7 display data. The submission includes 13 reports, 11 QUADAS-3 records, 10 PROBAST+AI records, and 35 GRADE profiles. CSVs support visualization only; source papers, raw ECG data, extraction forms, individual reviewer records, and patient data are deliberately excluded.
